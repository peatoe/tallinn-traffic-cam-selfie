about & privacy

tallinn cam selfie is a hobby project that turns Tallinn's public traffic cameras into selfie cameras: walk to one, start the countdown, wave. It is not affiliated with the City of Tallinn, Visit Estonia, or Brand Estonia.

data sources & credits

  • Camera images and camera list: served directly from the City of Tallinn's public traffic camera service ristmikud.tallinn.ee (Tallinna Liikuvusamet). Images are fetched by your browser straight from the city's server. This app does not store or redistribute them.
  • Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under ODbL. Tiles from openstreetmap.org.
  • Camera coordinates: derived from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and from Estonian Land Board (Maa-amet) in-ADS address data, 2026. The coordinate dataset is shared under ODbL.
  • Map library: Leaflet, BSD 2-Clause, © Vladimir Agafonkin.
  • Typefaces: Aino and Aino Headline by Brand Estonia (Estonian Business and Innovation Agency), used unmodified under their free licence. Design inspired by visitestonia.com.

privacy

  • Your location is used only in your browser to show you on the map and compute distances. It is never sent to this app's servers. there are none.
  • Your shots are stored only on your device (browser storage). Delete them anytime with the x. Your browser may evict them under storage pressure, so save keepers to your phone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies from this app. Your browser does contact two third parties: openstreetmap.org for map tiles and ristmikud.tallinn.ee for camera images. The city's server may set its own technical cookies; their service, their terms.
  • People in the frame: the cameras are the city's public traffic cameras. The city states recordings are kept at least 21 days and can be requested by law enforcement. Use your shots kindly. photograph yourself, not strangers.
