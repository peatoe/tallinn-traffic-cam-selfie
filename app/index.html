about & privacy

tallinn traffic cam selfie is a hobby project that turns Tallinn's public traffic cameras into selfie cameras: walk to one, start the countdown, wave. It is not affiliated with the City of Tallinn, Visit Estonia, or Brand Estonia.

support this project

free, no ads, no tracking. if it made you smile at a traffic camera, you can support it with a small donation. the button is a plain link. nothing loads until you tap it.

privacy

  • Your location is used only in your browser to show you on the map and compute distances. It is never sent to this app's servers. there are none.
  • Your shots are stored only on your device (browser storage). Delete them anytime with the x. Your browser may evict them under storage pressure, so save keepers to your phone. Browse them all on the your photos page up top.
  • Your favorites (starred cameras) are likewise stored only in this browser. Nothing about them leaves your device.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies from this app. Your browser does contact two third parties: openstreetmap.org for map tiles and ristmikud.tallinn.ee for camera images. The city's server may set its own technical cookies; their service, their terms.
  • People in the frame: the cameras are the city's public traffic cameras. The city states recordings are kept at least 21 days and can be requested by law enforcement. Use your shots kindly. photograph yourself, not strangers.

safety & respect

  • Photograph yourself, not strangers. Be kind to anyone who wanders into your frame.
  • Follow traffic and safety laws on the way there and while posing. Cross at crossings, obey signals, and never stand in the roadway, on medians, or among moving traffic for a shot.
  • No trespassing. Wave from public sidewalks and paths. Don't enter private property or fenced and restricted areas, and don't climb on road infrastructure.
  • Some map pins are approximate. Those cameras are marked on the camera page. Directions get you close; look around when you arrive.
  • Stay aware. Cars, trams, and bikes won't pose for you. Watch your surroundings. A photo is never worth an accident.
